# Visual QA criteria

A change is only correct if it satisfies these criteria.

## Homepage
- Does the first screen feel like a campaign image composition?
- Does the left side feel quieter than the right side?
- Does the split feel intentional rather than accidental?

## Navbar
- Does the logo feel truly centered?
- Does the nav feel expensive and sparse?
- Do the text anchors align to a clear system?

## Shop
- Does the page feel like a curated catalogue?
- Is the left rail strong enough?
- Does the product grid stop feeling like a centered block?

## Product cards
- Do cards feel quiet and image led?
- Is the text understated?
- Are hover behaviors restrained?

## Mobile
- Does the site still feel premium?
- Does anything feel cramped, generic, or broken?
- Is there any place where the desktop logic was merely squashed?
