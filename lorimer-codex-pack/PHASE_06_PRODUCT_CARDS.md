# Phase 06: Product card refinement

## Prompt

Refine the product cards so they match the tone of the Framer reference.

### Requirements

Each card should feel quiet and gallery like.

Structure:
- image
- title
- optional secondary line for status or availability

### Rules

- image is dominant
- text is secondary
- do not add boxes
- do not add shadows
- do not add border radius
- do not add commerce clutter
- no loud sale tags
- no heavy CTA buttons inside the card

### Hover behavior

Very restrained only:
- subtle image scale
- maybe subtle opacity or text emphasis
- no lift shadow
- no dramatic movement

### Typography

- title small and refined
- aligned with the overall editorial system
- status line should feel quiet and secondary

### Deliverable

Implement product card refinements and summarize:
- card anatomy
- hover behavior
- image aspect ratio handling
