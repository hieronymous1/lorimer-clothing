Cookie Policy

Lorimer keeps this website deliberately simple. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not build profiles of the people who visit.

What we store

Your cart: items you add are saved in your browser's local storage so your cart is still there when you come back. This stays on your device until you check out.
Cookie notice: we remember that you have closed the cookie notice so it is not shown again.
Both are strictly necessary for the website to work as you would expect, so they do not require consent.

Checkout

Payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure checkout page. Stripe may set cookies that are strictly necessary for payment security and fraud prevention. These are governed by Stripe's own privacy and cookie policy.

Fonts

Some typefaces on this website are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers. No cookies are set when this happens.

Managing your data

You can clear cookies and stored website data at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will empty your cart.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at contact@lorimer.com.
